How much does Book NBoosT cost?

Plans start from £45 per month, with cloud hosting included. Pay annually and use promo code XGTECH 20 to save 20%.

Can clients pay when they book?

Yes. Take full payment or a deposit at booking through gateways such as Stripe and PayPal, or let clients pay at the appointment, including by QR code.

Does it reduce no-shows?

Clients get automatic email reminders before their appointment, so fewer people forget or cancel at the last minute.

Can I add it to my website or social media?

Yes. Book NBoosT can be embedded in your existing website and linked from your social media pages, so clients book wherever they find you.

Will it work with my team?

Yes. Give staff different roles and access levels, assign bookings to staff members, track clock-ins and handle class substitutions from one dashboard.

Does it connect to my calendar and Zoom?

Yes. Book NBoosT integrates with Google Calendar and Zoom, so online meetings and existing calendars stay in sync.
